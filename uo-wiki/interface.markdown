# Interface and Shortcuts

The Rila HUD brings health, mana, level progress, spells and potions together beside the game world.

## Health, mana and progression

The red and blue orbs show health and mana. The progression display shows level, XP and unspent stat points. Click it to open stat allocation. Stamina stays full for living players and has no separate HUD orb.

## Spell shortcuts

- The four main slots default to **Q, W, E and R**. Right-click an icon to choose a classic Magery spell or supported combat ability.
- The two support slots default to **D and F**. They offer Heal, Greater Heal, Cure and Arch Cure and cast targeted healing or curing spells on yourself.
- Click a key label to change its binding. The client marks conflicts with other macros rather than overwriting them.
- Shortcuts do not cast while you are typing in chat or editing another input field.

The 64 classic Magery spells do not require or consume reagents in the level ruleset. Mana, targeting and other cast conditions still apply.

## Potion shortcuts

The four potion slots default to **1–4**. Right-click to choose a potion type; click or press the assigned key to use an actual potion from your backpack. Choices are saved per character.

Mana potions restore **30% of maximum mana** with a **10-second cooldown** under the current default rules. A potion is not consumed at full mana.

## Maps and communication

The world, world map and minimap share [persistent exploration]({{ '/exploration/' | relative_url }}). Use the [emote wheel]({{ '/emotes/' | relative_url }}) for short expressions above your character.
