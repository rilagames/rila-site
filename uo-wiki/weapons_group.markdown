# Special Weapons

The current catalog contains **36 special weapons** across ten families, plus the separate Mavi Hilal blade. Weapon speed depends on DEX; damage depends on the configured weapon and combat calculations.

## Requirements and durability

Each family page lists the current base damage, player-level requirement and STR requirement. Special weapons have unlimited durability. Matching names do not imply that a creature drops that weapon.

## Mavi Hilal

Mavi Hilal is the blue crescent combat ability. Select it in the skill tree and assign it to a main HUD slot. With a melee weapon equipped, choose a valid target; the next successful melee hit against that target within 10 seconds triggers the sweep.

The sweep covers a **120-degree forward sector within 7 tiles**. The primary target is not hit twice. Line of sight, height, friendly-party/guild and pet checks limit secondary targets. It deals physical damage subject to combat defenses.
