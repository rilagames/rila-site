# Party System

Use the standard party controls to invite players, accept invitations and leave a group.

## Shared kill experience

A creature has one XP budget for its defeat. Eligible players receive shares based on recorded damage contribution. Damage credited through pets counts toward their owner. A contributor's share is divided among eligible members of their party.

To receive XP you must be alive, connected, on the same map and within **18 tiles** of the defeated creature. You must also meet that creature's level requirement. Group membership does not bypass that requirement.

Party membership does not create a full XP reward for every player. Players outside the eligibility range receive no share from that kill.
