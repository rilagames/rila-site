NOTE
{: .label .label-red }

* Party invite,accept and quit are same as the original. /add /accept /quit

* Special addition is .party and .partyfinder.

* You can open your party for .partyfinder from .party dialog. Only party owners can use this button.

* If you are looking for a party, just type .partyfinder. There will be a list of open parties for you. You can join by clicking the button.

* Party members are sharing the experience. If you are in same region with the player who gains the experience; you will gain full experience with him. If you are not, you will gain half of the experience.
