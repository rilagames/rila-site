# Earth Weapons

Earth weapons add 30 damage against players and 50 against NPCs after a successful hit. A matching Earth suit blocks this extra damage.

![Earth Weapons artwork]({{ "/assets/img/weapons_earth.png" | relative_url }})

| Weapon | Base damage | Required level | Required STR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Earth Elemental Sword | 35 | 27 | 250 |
| Earth Elemental Bow | 35 | 27 | 250 |
| Earth Elemental Mace | 45 | 45 | 400 |
| Earth Elemental Halberd | 30 | 45 | 400 |
| Earth Elemental Crossbow | 50 | 33 | 75 |

Base damage is the configured weapon value before combat calculations. The server checks the required player level when equipping and attacking.

These special weapons have unlimited durability. Ranged weapons in these families do not consume arrows or bolts in the level ruleset. Acquisition routes are not listed here.
