# Equipment Durability

Rila's special equipment uses **unlimited durability** in the level ruleset. This applies to the cataloged special weapons, Mavi Hilal, Rila armor sets and their pieces or shields, and the eleven special accessories.

These items do not wear out, break or lose maximum durability through normal wear. The item tooltip identifies unlimited durability. Previously damaged special equipment does not lose its intended combat strength because of that old damage.

Ordinary equipment retains its own durability rules. Check the item tooltip to distinguish special Rila equipment from standard items.
