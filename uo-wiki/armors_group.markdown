# Armor suits

Rila armor is worn as **one item**. You do not collect or equip six separate armor pieces, and there are no MK upgrade tiers in the current system.

The screenshots and values below were checked in the live Rila client on **24 October 2026**. Armor values are for undamaged suits.

## Equipping and changing suits

- Double-click a suit in your own backpack to equip it. The previous suit moves to your backpack.
- The worn suit appears in the paperdoll's **Armor Suit Slot**. Drag it into your backpack to remove it.
- After a suit is equipped, there is a **4-second delay** before another suit can be equipped. Removing it does not reset this delay.
- In macro settings, choose **ArmorWear**, choose the suit and assign a key. The macro uses a matching suit in your backpack and follows the same server checks.
- Spells can be cast while wearing armor. Equipping a suit also shows a short effect in its color.

## Requirements and durability

The final wear levels are **to be decided**. The existing temporary maximum levels still apply: Beginner up to level **25**, and both Twix suits up to level **45**. The other current suits have no maximum-level restriction. Required STR and durability are listed on each suit page.

The armor table gives full-durability values. A damaged suit can show a lower armor value on the character status panel.

## Historical armor pages

Flame, Heaven and Holy are not included in the current live equipment catalog. Their pages are marked as unavailable; historical artwork and old bonus descriptions are not presented as current live data.
