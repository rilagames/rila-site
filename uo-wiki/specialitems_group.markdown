## Special hit effects

- **Zeus Gloves:** 25% chance of God's Damage, 7 base physical damage, reduced by armor.
- **Cloak of Displacement:** 1 in 7 chance to block a physical weapon/unarmed hit; never blocks spell or poison damage.
- **Cloak of Extra Damage:** 25% chance of 10 base extra physical damage, reduced by armor.

The two offensive effects require a successful weapon/unarmed hit that deals actual damage. They do not trigger from spell or poison damage.

## Equipment slots and availability

Choose one item per slot: Zeus' Boots or Khalim's Boots; Zeus Gloves or Gloves of Hercules; Bravering or Broteas mask; Notum or Holy robe; Displacement or Extra Damage cloak. Elven Belt occupies the belt slot. These accessories can be used alongside the one-piece Armor Suit when the ordinary equipment checks allow it.

No new creature drops or crafting recipes have been assigned to these accessories. Staff can inspect all entries in the **Rila özel eşyalar** menu, opened by `.RilaSpecialItems` / `.RilaOzelEsyalar` or **Staff → Dünya / eşya**.
