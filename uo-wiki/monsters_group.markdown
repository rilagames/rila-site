# Warriors

Rila currently has **seven configured warrior variants**: Angel, Devil, Fire, Earth, Arcane, Lightning and Acid. The table covers this warrior family; it is not a list of every monster and boss.

## Level requirements

All seven require **level 24 or higher** to attack. The server checks direct attacks, harmful spells, pet attacks and later damage. You cannot use a pet or an underlevel party member to bypass the requirement. The player level cap is **60**.

## Combat and experience

Warriors switch between a sword and shield at close range and a bow at longer range. They may also teleport toward a valid nearby attacker after taking damage. Their names distinguish variants; they do not grant the abilities of similarly named armor suits.

The XP column is one reward budget per kill, shared by eligible contributors and party members. It is not the reward for every person in the party. Players below the required level do not receive a share.

## Availability and drops

These entries describe the configured warrior catalog. Spawn locations are not published here. Existing world NPCs were left unchanged when this catalog was added.

No armor-suit or special-item drops are configured for the current warriors. NPC equipment cannot be worn by players. Vampiric Warrior is a historical entry and is not one of the seven current variants.

*Values checked against the current Rila server catalog on 24 October 2026.*
