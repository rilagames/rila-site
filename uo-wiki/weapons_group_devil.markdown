# Devil Weapons

Devil weapons add 15–25 damage against a target wearing an Angel suit.

![Devil Weapons artwork]({{ "/assets/img/weapons_devil.png" | relative_url }})

| Weapon | Base damage | Required level | Required STR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Devil Heavy Crossbow | 25 | 24 | 300 |
| Devil Bow | 20 | 18 | 250 |
| Devil Sword | 20 | 18 | 250 |
| Devil Mace | 25 | 18 | 300 |
| Devil Bardiche | 30 | 18 | 400 |

Base damage is the configured weapon value before combat calculations. The server checks the required player level when equipping and attacking.

These special weapons have unlimited durability. Ranged weapons in these families do not consume arrows or bolts in the level ruleset. Acquisition routes are not listed here.
