# Exploration

Reveal the world as you travel. Your discoveries are shared by the game view, world map and minimap, and remain saved for that character and map across reconnects.

## Named regions

Entering a named region reveals its defined area. Separate pieces of a region are revealed without filling the gaps between them. A region title appears on your first discovery of that region.

## Open terrain

Where no named region applies, exploration reveals an area of roughly **18 tiles** around you. Teleporting reveals your destination, not the path between the two locations.

## Trade Island

Trade Island is the named starting-town region in Felucca. The discovery name marks the town; it does not itself add combat protection or a transport service.
