# Stats and Regeneration

Build your character through level rewards and equipment. New level progression starts with **100 base STR, 100 base DEX and 100 base INT**.

## Base stat limits

| Stat | Base limit |
|---|---:|
| Strength | 400 |
| Dexterity | 200 |
| Intelligence | 100 |

Open the level window from the HUD to allocate earned stat points. Equipment can add bonuses beyond these base allocation limits. Dexterity still affects weapon speed.

## Natural regeneration

| Level | Health per 2 seconds | Mana per second |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–9 | 3 | 1 |
| 10–19 | 3.5 | 1⅙ |
| 20–29 | 4 | 1⅓ |
| 30–39 | 4.5 | 1½ |
| 40–49 | 5 | 1⅔ |
| 50–59 | 5.5 | 1⅚ |
| 60 | 6 | 2 |

Fractions accumulate between ticks. Regeneration does not build up while you are offline, dead or already at full health or mana. Poison can prevent health regeneration.

Each equipped point of health or mana regeneration adds **1 resource per 10 seconds**. Items carried in a backpack do not count as equipped. Angel armor's healing effect is included in the health rate shown by Character Status.

## Stamina and carrying

Living players keep full stamina. Running does not drain it, while DEX retains its combat role. The player backpack and containers inside it have no item-count or weight capacity barrier. Banks, world containers and house storage retain their own rules.
