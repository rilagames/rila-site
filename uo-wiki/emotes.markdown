# Emote Wheel

Hold the **middle mouse button** to open the wheel. Move toward an expression, then release the button to send it. Release near the center, press Escape or switch focus to cancel.

| Direction | Expression | Icon |
|---|---|---|
| Up | Laugh | ![Laughing orc]({{ '/assets/img/emote-0.png' | relative_url }}) |
| Right | Surprise / question | ![Surprised mage]({{ '/assets/img/emote-1.png' | relative_url }}) |
| Down | Heart | ![Knight with a heart]({{ '/assets/img/emote-2.png' | relative_url }}) |
| Left | Anger | ![Angry dragon]({{ '/assets/img/emote-3.png' | relative_url }}) |

The expression follows your character above their head and fades after about **3 seconds**. Nearby players who can see you receive it. You must be alive, and there is a **3-second cooldown** between uses.
