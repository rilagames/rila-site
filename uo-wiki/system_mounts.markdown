# Ethereal mounts

All **20 mount types** in the current Rila mount catalog are ethereal items. Carry one in your own backpack and double-click it to ride. When you dismount, **the same item returns to your backpack**; a new animal is not left behind.

## Riding and dismounting

You must be alive, unmounted and have one free follower slot. The item must be in your own backpack. You cannot activate it while paralyzed, frozen or casting. Standard region restrictions, mount blocks, trading and character-form restrictions still apply.

Dismount through the normal character controls to return the item to your backpack. On death, it also returns to item form. Mount state survives a world save and server restart.

Ethereal describes this item lifecycle. The mounts retain their normal artwork and color; the name does not imply that every dismount mechanic is disabled.

## Bonuses and durability

The current mounts **do not grant STR, DEX or INT bonuses**. They do not use the old wiki's 10/10 mount durability system. The catalog does not add a mount-specific level requirement; normal mounting restrictions still apply.
